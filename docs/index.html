01 / ACCESS

The link is the invitation.

Private 1:1 conversations or limited groups. Add an optional password and share it separately for another layer of admission control.

02 / ENCRYPTION

Your words stay between you.

Messages are encrypted on the client before they reach the server. Verify peer key fingerprints through a separate trusted channel.

03 / BOUNDARIES

Full means closed.

A room seals when its participant limit is reached. Existing participants can reconnect without taking a new slot. Previews never claim a place.

04 / RETENTION

No permanent server history.

Pending encrypted messages are removed after acknowledgment, read, or expiry. Participants may retain local history on their own devices.

From link to conversation

Three steps. Then it’s yours.

NO PUBLIC DIRECTORY
  1. 01

    Create a room.

    On your Sideword server, choose a personal or group invite. Set an expiry, participant limit, and optional password.

  2. 02

    Share the invitation.

    Send the link to the people you choose. If protected, send the password separately. Joining is an explicit action.

  3. 03

    Talk. Let access expire.

    Verify each other’s keys and exchange encrypted messages. When an invite expires, access ends—even for saved sessions.

SECURITY, IN PLAIN WORDS

Passwords control admission; they are not encryption keys. Password protection requires trusting the server and its HTTPS terminator. Encryption does not hide all metadata.
